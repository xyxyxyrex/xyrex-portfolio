## Final prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Generate one polished desktop website mockup of a CODE PROJECTS section for Xyrex Kyle Salazar's creative developer portfolio. The attached reference is a multi-tenant roadside shopping-plaza directory / advertisement sign. Use its design language of compact stacked sign panels of varying dimensions, tight structural framing, and distinctive typography. Ignore all existing business names, photographed surroundings, meme captions and video overlays in the reference. This is a new flat website design, not a photo of a real sign.
Create a visually compelling DIGITAL PROJECT SIGNBOARD: a dense, interlocking grid of six advertisement-sign panels with varied widths/heights, inside a slim dark metal-like rectangular framework. It should feel like walking past a plaza directory translated into a graphic design portfolio. Warm pale-gray #eeede8 webpage background, near-black framework, tiny 8px dark gutters, sharp corners, tactile subtle printed texture, restrained weathering, bold typographic identities. No rounded bento cards, no separated floating cards, no drop-shadow stacks, no 3D camera perspective. Large landscape straight-on website screenshot 1600x1000 or similar.
At upper left, small monospace "CODE / SELECTED WORK"; upper right "06 PROJECTS"; restrained title "</project directory>" in bold monospace above the signboard, keep heading compact to prioritize the signboard. Narrow little caption "A few things I've built." near header.
All six projects must have separate, legible sign panels with their exact titles and small platform labels:
1 "NMCG" — forest-green and cream elongated horizontal sign, botanical emblem, small "MANGROVE CONSERVATION", "WEB + MOBILE".
2 "CET E-BULLETIN BOARD" — tall compact cream-and-red typographic sign with stacked condensed lettering, tiny "CAMPUS ANNOUNCEMENTS", "WEB".
3 "MEMORA-AI" — larger navy-blue/violet sign, expressive white oversized name and small cyan highlight; "YOUR LEARNING COMPANION", "REACT / PYTHON".
4 "CAREFIND" — cream sign with confident near-black letters, muted green health cross, tiny "CARE, CONNECTED.", "WEB + MOBILE".
5 "TONO" — compact electric-blue and cream horizontal sign with expressive italic typography, tiny "NOTES ROOTED IN PLACE", "WEB".
6 "THRIFTLY" — wider warm-yellow and near-black sign with bold friendly lettering, small reuse arrow symbol; "SECONDHAND. SECOND LIFE.", "WEB + MOBILE".
Mix these real project names as if each were an individual tenant advertisement, while retaining the overall portfolio composition. A four-column modular layout can use: NMCG across top-left two cells; MEMORA-AI in a tall panel occupying upper-right two columns/two rows; CET as a narrow tall panel bottom-left; CAREFIND as a square center-left; THRIFTLY wide along lower middle; TONO as a short strip along lower right. Resolve exact placements into a tight filled rectangular mosaic with no blank holes. Panels should vary clearly in aspect ratio, visual rhythm, and letter treatment.
Include tiny monospace project numbers 01–06 discreetly in each sign, and small diagonal arrows in corners suggesting they open project details. Show one hovered panel, Memora-AI, with a subtle lit inset border and a little attached ticker reading "OPEN PROJECT ↗"; avoid covering its title. Keep all information readable at normal desktop viewing scale. No fake statistics, reviews, ratings, awards or clients. No large app screenshots: these signs themselves are the navigation, opening projects on click. Small gray text under the board: "Choose a sign to explore a project."
At bottom center, a compact floating rounded-rectangle frosted glass dock with five thin-line icons: home, person, code active, image, envelope, consistent with an iOS-inspired portfolio dock. The page should feel stylish, compact, playful, and art-directed, with screen-print signage references and strong hierarchy rather than generic SaaS UI.
